Safety Guidelines & Facility Information
⚠ MUST READ

Important Notice Before Check-In

Please review the following before your training begins.

🚫
No Alcohol
Not permitted at check-in or during training · No outings
🚫 NO ALCOHOL

You may not check in while intoxicated, and alcohol consumption and outings are not permitted during the training period.
※ Violations will result in dismissal and formal reporting.

🚭
No Smoking
All facilities are non-smoking areas
🚭 NO SMOKING

All on-site facilities are non-smoking areas.
Smoking is only permitted in designated areas.

🍱
No Outside Food
Outside food is restricted
🍱 NO OUTSIDE FOOD

Bringing in outside food is restricted.
This helps prevent foodborne illness and safety incidents during group living.

📵
No Phone Use While Walking
Walk safely
📵 NO PHONE WHILE WALKING

Using your phone while walking is prohibited.
Please stop and stand still before using your phone.

🚪
Check Emergency Exit Locations
Be prepared in advance
✅ Required After Check-In

Please locate the nearest emergency exit after checking in.
In an emergency, move quickly to a safe evacuation area.

📢
Listen for Emergency Announcements
Follow emergency instructions
📢 Stay Alert

In an emergency, listen carefully to announcements.
Please remain calm and follow the instructions given.

🎒
Manage Personal Belongings
Watch for loss or damage
🎒 Your Responsibility

Please keep your personal belongings with you at all times in the training venue or accommodation.
Be careful to avoid loss or damage.

⚡
Report Safety Hazards
Use the Safety Hotline
⚡ Report Immediately

If you notice a safety hazard, please report it immediately to your training coordinator or the Safety Hotline.

📱 [Search KakaoTalk Channel] LG Electronics (Pyeongtaek) Safety Hotline

📷
No Personal Storage Devices / Cameras
Not permitted on-site
📷 NO STORAGE DEVICES / CAMERAS

Personal storage devices (USB drives, external hard drives, etc.) and cameras may not be brought on-site.
Please leave these items at home before arrival.

LG Digital Park Fall Accident Prevention
Real Case Examples · Prevention Measures
📋 Case Examples
🍺
Fell walking home after a dinner
Sep 8, 2023
An employee fell on a walkway while walking home intoxicated after a department dinner, fracturing two front teeth
🩴
Fell while walking down a slope
Sep 4, 2023
A partner company employee wearing Crocs (sandals) fell while walking down a slope, tearing a ligament in their left ankle
🌧️
Fell while running in the rain
Aug 28, 2023
An employee wearing sandals tripped on their pant leg while running in the rain, fracturing a toe on their right foot
✅ Prevention Measures
🍷 Avoid excessive drinking at dinners
🩴 Avoid slippery footwear like sandals
🌧️ Watch for slippery surfaces in rain/snow
🤲 Don't walk with hands in pockets
📵 No phone use while walking
🚶 Don't rush or run
🚨 Emergency Reporting
Any on-site accident (non-work-related) must be reported within 1 hour.
